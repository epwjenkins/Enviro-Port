Aggregate Recovery Series

Our Aggregate Recovery Series uses a conventional weir or filter-media system to dewater portland cement fines: a straightforward, affordable way to recover sand and stone in succession with settling ponds. It's the workhorse choice for yards that want reliable aggregate recovery without a full water-reclamation build-out.

  • 15–25 cubic yards / hour capacity
  • Multiple truck discharge points
  • Material-on-material design: no wear on tank floor or screw housing
  • Pairs with existing settling ponds
  • Heavy-duty screw conveyor construction
  • Lower upfront cost, fast payback

Complete Recovery Series

The Complete Recovery Series takes reclamation further, capturing fine and coarse aggregate for resale or reuse while routing reclaimed gray water directly back into new batches. State-of-the-art control systems monitor the process automatically, so operators spend less time managing the reclaimer and more time running the plant.

  • Gray water reincorporated into new ready-mix batches
  • Automated, state-of-the-art control system
  • Captures fine and coarse aggregate for resale
  • Configurable per-site, including the higher-capacity 5500 model
  • Reduces or eliminates dependence on settling ponds
  • Best fit for high-volume or environmentally-restricted sites

Components for retrofits and custom systems

Already have part of a reclamation setup? Every major component is available on its own, engineered to integrate with an existing system or anchor a fully custom build.

Intake Hoppers

Heavy-gauge steel hoppers sized to accept full drum discharge without splash-back or overflow.

Screw Conveyors

Material-on-material wear design carries separated sand and stone out of the slurry with minimal maintenance.

Settling & Agitator Tanks

Weir-style and agitated tank options for cement-fines settling and continuous gray water suspension.

Control Packages

Automated monitoring and control panels for water level, agitation cycles, and system diagnostics.

Storm & Process Water Management

Components engineered to help manage site storm water and process water alongside your reclamation flow.

Wear Parts & Service

Replacement flighting, liners, and seals to keep an existing reclaimer running at full capacity.
