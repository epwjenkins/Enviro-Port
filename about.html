Built in Wisconsin, running in yards nationwide

Enviro-Port designs and manufactures concrete reclamation equipment out of Gratiot, Wisconsin, with one focus: helping ready-mix and precast producers recover material that would otherwise be wasted.

Our Mission

Less waste on the ground, more material back in the mix

Every year, ready-mix producers across the country wash out millions of tons of usable aggregate and dump it along with the water used to clean drums and chutes. That's recoverable material, and recoverable revenue, left to hit a settling pond or harden in a pile.

Enviro-Port exists to close that gap. We engineer reclaimer systems that separate returned concrete into clean aggregate and reusable water on-site, so producers can recover material value while reducing the environmental footprint of their operation.

Why Producers Choose Us

Backed by a Dealer Network

What We Focus On

Three things, done well

Aggregate Recovery

Separating sand and stone from returned mix so it can be resold or reused instead of dumped and hardened.

Water Reclamation

Routing reclaimed gray water back into new batches, reducing fresh-water demand and pond dependence.

Environmental Compliance

Helping producers manage process and storm water on-site as part of responsible plant operations.
