The Problem We Solve

Every unused yard of returned concrete is money hardening in a pile.

Across the U.S., ready-mix producers wash out and discard millions of tons of usable aggregate and water every year. Enviro-Port reclaimers intercept that returned mix before it hardens, separating sand and stone for reuse and keeping wash water in a closed loop instead of a settling pond.

  • Recover clean, sellable aggregate from every returned load
  • Reclaim gray water for reuse directly in new batches
  • Reduce settling-pond dredging and storm water compliance risk

The Process

From returned load to recovered material in four steps

01

Intake

Returned ready-mix is poured directly into the intake hopper from the drum.

02

Separation

The screw conveyor carries sand and stone out of the slurry, mechanically separated on the way.

03

Settling

Gray water flows into the weir settling system, where cement fines drop out of suspension.

04

Reuse

Recovered aggregate is stockpiled for resale; reclaimed water is routed back into the batch plant.
